Cauvery River Row: Supreme Court Rejects Tamil Nadu’s 11 TMC Plea | Karnataka Reacts | News18

INDIA The Supreme Court has dismissed Tamil Nadu's pleas seeking 11 TMC of Cauvery water from Karnataka. The Supreme Court on Friday dismissed two applications filed by the Tamil Nadu government seeking directions to Karnataka to release 11 thousand million cubic feet (TMC) of Cauvery river water to the state, dealing a setback to its efforts to recover the alleged shortfall in water supply. The Karnataka government had told the top court that it would be difficult to meet Tamil Nadu's demand for the release of 11 TMC feet of Cauvery river water for making good a "shortfall" as this was a rain-deficit year and several areas have been declared drought-affected.

Copyright & Content Takedown Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

TrippyNews is committed to operating in full compliance with copyright law, digital privacy rights, and ethical web standards. We respect intellectual property and provide a rapid, accountable mechanism to request removal or suppression of content.

48-Hour SLA Commitment: All valid takedown, copyright infringement, or defamation challenge requests submitted via our dedicated channel are reviewed and actioned within 48 hours of receipt.

Our Principles & Safeguards

  • No Verbatim Article Republication: TrippyNews never republishes full source articles or RSS snippets. All summaries are independently synthesized in our own words from corroborated facts.
  • Strictly Capped Evidence Quotes: Direct quotations are limited to short evidentiary snippets of no more than 25 words, accompanied by prominent outlet attribution and outbound hyperlinks directly to the original publisher.
  • Robots.txt & Ingestion Opt-Out: Publishers can control or opt out of indexing at any time via standard robots.txt directives or by requesting a permanent domain blocklist entry.
  • Dual-Stage Blocklist: Our pipeline enforces an automated blocklist (blocklist.yaml) active at both the ingestion stage and the static site build stage. Blocklisted URLs, domains, or stories are instantly purged from the live site.

How to Submit a Notice

To submit a copyright, privacy, or correction takedown notice, send an email to psection189@gmail.com with the subject line Content Takedown Request including:

  1. Specific URL(s): The exact URL(s) on TrippyNews where the disputed content appears.
  2. Original Work / Basis of Claim: Identification of the copyrighted work, court order, or privacy basis for the request.
  3. Your Authority: Evidence or a statement that you are the rights holder, named individual, or authorized representative.
  4. Requested Action: Removal of an evidence quote, suppression of a story cluster, or permanent blocklisting of a domain/feed.

Action & Public Transparency

Upon verification, the requested item is immediately added to the pipeline blocklist. During the next automated run (or immediate manual trigger), the content is removed from all public pages, and a record of the update is recorded in our Public Changelog.

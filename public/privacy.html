Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Privacy-First Commitment

TrippyNews is built with strict privacy principles. We do not track individual users across the web, do not serve advertisements, do not collect personal identifiers, and do not use invasive tracking technologies.

2. Visitor Analytics (GoatCounter)

To measure overall website traffic and footfall, TrippyNews uses GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-preserving web analytics service. GoatCounter operates with the following privacy guarantees:

  • No Cookies: GoatCounter does not set or read any HTTP cookies on your device.
  • No Personal Data: No personally identifiable information (such as IP addresses, names, or email addresses) is ever collected or stored.
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: Visitor sessions cannot be tracked across different websites.
  • Aggregated Metrics Only: Only high-level aggregated metrics (such as page visit counts, approximate country of origin, and browser platform) are recorded to help us understand site usage.

For more details on GoatCounter's privacy architecture, see the official GoatCounter Privacy Policy.

3. Static Hosting & GitHub Pages

This website is hosted statically via GitHub Pages. When you request a page, GitHub's edge network may record standard HTTP transmission logs (including requesting IP and User-Agent) as part of infrastructure operations and security. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at contact@trippynews.org.

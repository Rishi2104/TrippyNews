Methodology & Transparency Policy

TrippyNews is a fully automated, algorithmic news corroboration pipeline. It operates without manual editorial curation, political slant, or subjective ranking. Every metric and label displayed on this website is computed mathematically from source diversity, independent reporting tiers, and semantic consensus.

1. Independence & Wire Deduplication

Wire agencies (such as Press Trust of India, ANI, Reuters, Associated Press, AFP) syndicate their reporting across hundreds of partner newspapers and broadcasters. Ten outlets syndicating the exact same wire story count as ONE confirmation, not ten.

Our pipeline analyzes syndication credits, wire markers, and n-gram shingles across articles. All syndicated copies are clustered and mapped to their primary wire origin. A story is only considered corroborated when multiple independent newsrooms independently verify the reporting.

2. Scoring Formula & Mathematical Metrics

Every story cluster is scored using a calibrated mathematical formula defined by our pipeline constants:

  • Tier 1 Sources (Wire Services & Official Bureaus): 3.0 points per independent outlet.
  • Tier 2 Sources (Major National & International Newsrooms): 2.0 points per independent outlet.
  • Tier 3 Sources (Specialized & Regional Outlets): 1.0 point per independent outlet.
  • Primary / Official Source Bonus: +2.0 points if verified by a Tier 1 wire or official press bureau.
  • Conflict Penalty: -0.3 points for each unresolved factual contradiction detected between reporting outlets.
  • Recency Factor: A continuous decay function applies over time (with a half-decay window of 6.0 hours), ensuring recent developing reporting is prioritized.

The final corroboration score is computed as:

Score = max(0.0, (Base Tier Points + Primary Bonus - Dispute Penalty) × Recency Factor)

This continuous recency scaling explains fractional corroboration scores (such as 3.98, 5.98, 7.03, 7.88, 10.92) as stories mature over hours while maintaining their underlying source strength.

3. Corroboration Labels & Strict Thresholds

  • Strongly Corroborated: Requires at least 4+ independent sources, a corroboration score of ≥ 8.0, inclusion of at least one Tier 1 wire or official source, and zero major contradictions.
  • Corroborated: Requires at least 3+ independent sources, a score of ≥ 4.5, and consistent factual reporting across outlets.
  • Limited Corroboration: Stories with 1–2 sources or with significant unresolved factual disputes between reporting outlets.
  • Unconfirmed: Fewer than 3 independent sources. AI narrative synthesis is strictly suppressed.

4. AI Narrative Gate & Hallucination Prevention

To eliminate AI hallucinations, TrippyNews does not allow an LLM to freely invent narrative text. A natural language summary is synthesized ONLY when all of the following conditions are met:

  1. The story is corroborated by at least 3 independent sources.
  2. At least 2 distinct factual claims are corroborated across multiple independent outlets.
  3. The synthesized summary strictly entails the headline and key named entities. If entailment fails, the summary is suppressed.
  4. The topic is not flagged as sensitive under our Defamation & Legal Safeguard policy.

If any condition is not met, the narrative summary is omitted, and the story page displays the structured claim comparison table directly.

5. Defamation & Legal Safeguards

For reporting that involves court proceedings, ongoing trials, allegations against named private individuals, or crimes, our pipeline activates a strict Claims-Only Defamation Guard. In this mode:

  • Narrative generation is entirely disabled.
  • Every factual assertion is explicitly attributed with Per <outlet>: prefixes.
  • Conflicting claims or differing statements from legal parties are displayed side-by-side with both outlets named.

6. Vetted Ingestion & Domain Whitelisting

All feeds are whitelisted in sources.yaml. During feed fetching, article URLs are strictly checked against per-source allowed_domains. Unvetted third-party domains, redirect loops, and tracking parameters (such as utm_* and traffic_source) are stripped or discarded.

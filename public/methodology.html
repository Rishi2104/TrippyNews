Methodology & Transparency Policy

NewsCheck is a fully automated news corroboration pipeline. It operates without human curation, editor bias, or manual publishing approvals.

1. Independence & Wire Deduplication

Wire services (PTI, ANI, Reuters, AP, AFP) are syndicated across hundreds of outlets. Ten outlets republishing the exact same wire story count as ONE confirmation, not ten. Our pipeline detects syndication credits and text shingles to collapse syndicated reprints into single canonical events.

2. Corroboration Scoring Formula

The corroboration score is computed mathematically:

  • Tier 1 Sources (Wire services, government press bureaus): 3.0 points
  • Tier 2 Sources (Major reputable national publications): 2.0 points
  • Tier 3 Sources (Other whitelisted publications): 1.0 point
  • Primary / Official Source Bonus: +2.0 points if verified primary source reported the event
  • Conflict Penalty: Score is reduced when contradictory figures or assertions are detected

3. Labels

  • Strongly Corroborated: 4+ independent sources including Tier 1, no significant contradictions.
  • Corroborated: 3+ independent sources in consensus.
  • Limited Corroboration: Few sources or unresolved conflicts between reporting outlets.
  • Unconfirmed: Fewer than 3 independent sources. No generated narrative is published.

4. Sensitive Topic Policy

When reporting on crimes naming private individuals, health or medical claims, suicide, minors, communal violence, or active armed conflicts, NewsCheck suppresses all generated narrative. Only a verbatim claim comparison table and direct source links are displayed.

5. Source Whitelist

All feeds are vetted and whitelisted in sources.yaml. Unvetted third-party domains are discarded during ingestion.

Up to 90% corpus withdrawal allowed in major PF reform

The minimum waiting period for making withdrawals under Employee Pension Scheme (EPS), however, remains unchanged at 36 months. Around 6.3 lakh EPF members aged above 55 years with provident fund accumulations of over Rs 5 lakh are expected to be primary beneficiaries of the proposed SWP framework. Around 632,000 EPF members aged above 55 years with provident fund savings exceeding ₹5 lakh are pegged to be the primary beneficiaries in the initial phase, a press release said. The board also reduced the minimum balance that members must retain in their PF accounts from 25% to 10% of their contributions. Members will now be required to retain only 10% of their contributions in their accounts at all times. - The waiting period for withdrawal has also been shortened from 12 months to two months.

PUC certificates to last longer for newer BS-VI cars, shorter for older ones The revised rules extend PUC certificate validity to three years for newer private BS-VI vehicles, while those older than 10 years will need renewals every six months. Those aged more than six years but up to 10 years will need annual renewal, while vehicles older than 10 years must renew their certificates every six months. Those up to six years old will require renewal once every two years, while vehicles aged more than six years but up to 10 years will need annual renewal.
